# ArtisanHub - Creative Arts Skill Development Hub

A blockchain-based creative arts skill development and mastery rewards platform built on Stacks, fostering artistic growth through transparent skill tracking and achievement recognition.

## Overview

ArtisanHub enables artists to develop their creative skills in recognized disciplines while earning mastery rewards based on their artistic contributions, promoting cultural enrichment and creative expression.

## Features

- Creative skill logging with art discipline verification
- Recognized art discipline management system
- Mastery reward calculation and distribution
- Transparent artistic development tracking and rewards
- Arts mentor oversight and governance

## Smart Contract Functions

### Public Functions
- `establish-artisan-hub`: Initialize creative arts development hub
- `recognize-art-discipline`: Recognize art disciplines for skill tracking
- `record-skill-development`: Record skill development with art discipline
- `evaluate-mastery-rewards`: Evaluate artistic mastery rewards
- `complete-mastery-certification`: Complete certification and claim rewards

### Read-Only Functions
- `get-artist-skills`: Get artist's total skill points
- `get-art-discipline`: Get artist's art discipline
- `get-total-creativity-points`: Get total creativity points
- `is-discipline-recognized`: Check art discipline recognition status

## Usage

Deploy the contract and initialize with an arts mentor. Recognize art disciplines, then artists can record skill development and complete certifications to claim rewards.

## Security

- Arts mentor authorization controls
- Art discipline recognition system for verified tracking
- Input validation for all skill development entries
- Artistic progress verification before reward distribution
